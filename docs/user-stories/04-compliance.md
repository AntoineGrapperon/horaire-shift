# User Stories: Compliance & Safety

## US.7 - Mandatory Rest Periods
**As a** Compliance Officer  
**I want to** prevent the solver (or manager) from assigning shifts that result in less than 11 hours of rest between work periods  
**So that** we comply with labor laws and reduce fatigue-related risks.

## US.8 - Fairness Logic
**As a** Compliance Officer / Clinician  
**I want to** track the distribution of "unpopular" shifts (nights, weekends, holidays) over a year  
**So that** I can ensure no single staff member is disproportionately burdened.

## US.9 - Staff Profiles & Contracts
**As an** Admin  
**I want to** maintain accurate records of staff contract hours and seniority  
**So that** the solver respects individual work-time agreements.
