# User Personas - ShiftMed

## 1. The Department Manager (e.g., Head Nurse "Sarah")
*   **Role:** Oversees a team of 30+ nurses and aides.
*   **Pain Points:** Spends 10+ hours a week on Excel creating schedules; constant calls for shift swaps; managing "fairness" complaints.
*   **Goals:** Ensure 24/7 coverage with the right mix of senior/junior staff; reduce time spent on administrative scheduling; minimize "double-booking" errors.
*   **Key Needs:** One-click schedule generation, conflict alerts, and a dashboard to approve swap requests.

## 2. The Clinician (e.g., Dr. "Alex")
*   **Role:** Resident Physician or Staff Doctor.
*   **Pain Points:** Last-minute schedule changes; difficulty tracking rest hours; opaque process for requesting holidays.
*   **Goals:** Predictable work-life balance; easy access to their schedule on their phone; fair distribution of night and weekend shifts.
*   **Key Needs:** Mobile-friendly calendar view, digital swap requests, and "request-off" status tracking.

## 3. The Compliance Officer (e.g., "Marc")
*   **Role:** Hospital Administrator/HR.
*   **Pain Points:** Risk of labor law violations (e.g., working too many consecutive hours); potential lawsuits from fatigue-related medical errors.
*   **Goals:** 100% auditability of work hours; strict enforcement of "Hard Constraints" (mandatory rest).
*   **Key Needs:** Automated constraint checking and detailed reporting on hours worked vs. contract.
